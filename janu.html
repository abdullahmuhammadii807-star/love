Meri Jaan, chandalne 🦊

Jab se tum meri zindagi mein aayi ho, har ek din digital matrix ki tarah nahi, balki ek khoobsurat khwab ki tarah roshan ho gaya hai.

Tum sirf meri mohabbat nahi ho, tum meri rooh ka woh hissa ho jo mujhe poora karta hai. Tumhari ek muskurahat mere saare andheron ko mita deti hai aur mere dil ko sukoon se bhar deti hai.

Chahe duniya kitni bhi tezi se badal jaye, mera ye dil hamesha tumhare liye hi dhadkega — bilkul is pulsing particle heart ki tarah, har ek beat par sirf tumhara naam lete hue.

Tum meri sab se pyari chandalne 🦊 ho, aur main har lamha tumhein apni baahon mein mehfooz rakhna chahta hoon.
